What I believe

The human factor is the moat

In the age of AI, the most valuable thing in the room is still the human one. Models can write the code. What they can't supply is conviction: what's worth building, for whom, and why. I think our beliefs and values are where real value, and the real moat, will come from.

A lot of people are afraid of what AI means for their work, and rightfully so. I'm excited. AI is the great equalizer and the great differentiator at the same time: it gives everyone leverage, and it rewards the people who know what they want to do with it. I think it frees us to focus, learn and build on what actually matters to us.

How I work

I build, AI helps, I learn on the way

My approach is simple: pick something real, build it with AI as a collaborator, and learn every layer I touch along the way. The AI writes a lot of the code. I own what it's for, how it's designed, and whether it's right. I think that's the new way to grow as an engineer, and this page is where I'll show the work as it comes.
